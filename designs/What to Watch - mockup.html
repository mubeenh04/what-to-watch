What to Watch

Clickable mockup for Android and iOS. Everything on the phone works. Each title lives in exactly one place, and every button moves it to the next one.

Explore→Watchlist→Home→History
  • Home. What you can watch now, on streaming or your drives. Filter by year, genre, location and scores. Actions: Watched (moves to History) or Hide.
  • Explore. Recommendations that aren't on your services or drives. Add to Watchlist, mark Watched, or Remove for good (it never comes back).
  • Watchlist. Movies and Series to download or stream. Mark downloaded moves the title to Home. History lists what you've watched, with a link to rate it on Letterboxd.

Sample titles, scores and dates for layout only. Service marks are plain placeholders, not the real logos. Reload the page to reset.
